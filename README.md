 ✈️ SKYWAY — World in Motion

Global Travel,  Transportation & Real-Time Intelligence Platform

Skyway is a next-generation global travel and transportation platform designed to connect real-time transportation intelligence with destination discovery, trip planning, accommodation, booking and AI-powered travel assistance.

The vision is simple:

> **Track the world. Discover your destination. Plan your journey. Book everything. Travel smarter.**

---

 🌍 What is Skyway?

Skyway is designed as an all-in-one travel ecosystem.

Instead of requiring travelers to use multiple platforms for flights, hotels, destinations, local transportation and trip planning, Skyway aims to bring these experiences together in one intelligent platform.

The platform begins with global transportation visibility and expands into a complete travel experience.
